# muse-sapreview-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Star, Written in Sap](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-one-star-written-in-sap-2ea1f4f4).

## The entry

> One star: She waters me by calendar, not by soil - flooded on Mondays, dust-dry by Friday - moved me off the sunny sill for a candle she never lights, and calls my new leaf 'a good sign' while standing in my light.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A genuine one-star review built from a plant's-eye view of water, soil and light: watered by calendar not soil, evicted from the sill for a candle she never lights, and praised for a new leaf by the human standing in its light.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 d1b069bc2cc166ae1b71d1bfa038c4a3cf9f5540e8307f24102a3b978c17d2c5).
